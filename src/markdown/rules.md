## Rules 🍿

### General Rules
---
1. Lineups consist of 1QB, 2RB, 2WR, 1TE, 1FLEX, 1K, 1DST.
1. All lineups must be fully set each week. Lineups with unset positions will be filled with the highest projected eligible player with respect to points. Exceptions will be made under certain circumstances with commissioner approval (e.g. BYE week with no eligible or droppable players on roster).
3. Any individual position in a lineup may be changed up until that position's the last eligible player begins their game.
4. Fantasy teams controlled are limited to ONE (1) per manager.
5. Each team's entry fee must be paid to the commissioner before the day of the draft.
6. All violations must be reported to the commissioner if otherwise unacknowledged.

<br />

### Trade Rules
---
1. Any player can initiate a trade.
2. Any player can bring forth a trade dispute in regard to a potential trade by alerting the commissioner.
3. Trades must be player(s)-for-player(s) exchanges only, no other forms of compensation will be tolerated.
4. Repeated infractions will result in a season-long suspension of roster changes for all teams involved in the vetoed trade.
5. When a dispute is opened, all players will have the opportunity to provide input and any missed context to the commissioner.
6. The commissioner has final approval over all disputed trades.
