### Article I — General League Rules

1. **Roster Composition.** Each team's starting lineup shall consist of the following positions: 1 Quarterback (QB), 2 Running Backs (RB), 2 Wide Receivers (WR), 1 Tight End (TE), 1 Flex (RB/WR/TE), 1 Kicker (K), and 1 Defense/Special Teams (DST).
2. **Lineup Deadlines.** All lineups must be fully and validly set prior to the start of each week's applicable games. Any lineup left incomplete or containing an ineligible player will automatically be populated with the highest-projected eligible player at that position. Exceptions to this rule may be granted solely at the discretion of the Commissioner (e.g., bye weeks with no eligible or droppable replacement players on the roster).
3. **In-Week Substitutions.** A player may be substituted in or out of a starting lineup position at any point up until the scheduled kickoff of that player's game. Once a player's game has begun, that lineup position is locked for the remainder of the week.
4. **One Team Per Manager.** Each league manager may own and control no more than one (1) team. Managing multiple teams under different identities, or on behalf of another person for the purpose of circumventing this rule, is strictly prohibited.
5. **Entry Fees.** All entry fees must be paid in full to the Commissioner prior to the start of the league draft. Managers who have not paid by the draft may be excluded from participation until payment is received.

7. **Reporting Violations.** Any suspected violation of league rules must be reported to the Commissioner in a timely manner. Failure to report a known violation may itself be treated as a violation.
8. **Rule Amendments.** These rules may be amended only with a majority vote of league managers, or at the sole discretion of the Commissioner in cases of ambiguity, exploitation, or unforeseen circumstance not addressed herein.

### Article IV — Trade Rules

1. **Trade Eligibility.** Any manager may propose a trade to any other manager at any time during the designated trading window of the season.
2. **Trade Composition.** Trades must consist exclusively of player(s)-for-player(s) exchanges (and, if permitted, draft pick(s)-for-draft pick(s), or a combination thereof). No other form of compensation — including but not limited to cash, FAAB dollars, or non-league consideration — will be permitted or recognized.
3. **Trade Deadline.** All trades must be submitted and finalized no later than [WEEK/DATE] of the regular season. No trades will be processed after this deadline.
4. **Trade Review Period.** All proposed trades are subject to a review period of [X] hours/days following acceptance by both parties, during which any manager may raise a formal dispute.
5. **Dispute Process.** Any manager may bring forth a trade dispute by formally alerting the Commissioner in writing. Upon a dispute being raised, all league managers shall be given a reasonable opportunity to provide input or relevant context before a decision is made.
6. **Commissioner Authority.** The Commissioner holds final and binding authority over all disputed trades, including the power to approve, veto, or reverse a trade found to be collusive, lopsided in bad faith, or otherwise contrary to the spirit of fair competition.
7. **Repeat Infractions.** Managers found to have repeatedly engaged in collusive or bad-faith trading may be subject to a season-long suspension of roster and trading privileges, at the Commissioner's discretion.

### Article VII — Conduct & Sportsmanship

1. **Good Faith Participation.** All managers are expected to set competitive lineups and manage their rosters in good faith for the duration of the season, including in games that do not affect their own playoff standing.
2. **Tanking Prohibited.** Intentionally fielding a non-competitive lineup to manipulate standings, opponents' schedules, or draft positioning is prohibited and may be reviewed by the Commissioner.
3. **Respectful Communication.** All league communication shall remain respectful. Harassment, personal attacks, or abusive conduct toward other managers may result in warnings, suspension, or removal from the league at the Commissioner's discretion.
4. **Collusion Prohibited.** Any agreement between managers to manipulate trades, waivers, lineups, or outcomes for mutual benefit outside the bounds of fair competition is strictly prohibited and subject to the penalties described in Article IV.

### Article VIII — Commissioner Authority & League Administration

1. **General Authority.** The Commissioner is responsible for the day-to-day administration of the league, including enforcement of these rules, resolution of disputes, and management of league finances.
2. **Impartiality.** The Commissioner shall act impartially in all rulings, including those involving their own team. Disputes involving the Commissioner's own team may be escalated to a vote of the remaining managers.
3. **Emergency Rulings.** In circumstances not explicitly addressed by this Constitution, the Commissioner may issue a temporary ruling, which shall be documented and subject to formal review and potential amendment at the next off-season league meeting.
