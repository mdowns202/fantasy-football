# Rulebook 📜

*Effective for the 2026 Season*

<br />

---

<br />

### Article I — General League Rules

1. **Roster Composition.** Each team's starting lineup shall consist of the following positions: 1 Quarterback (QB), 2 Running Backs (RB), 2 Wide Receivers (WR), 1 Tight End (TE), 1 Flex (RB/WR/TE), 1 Kicker (K), and 1 Defense/Special Teams (DST).
2. **Lineup Deadlines.** All lineups must be fully and validly set prior to the start of each week's applicable games. Any lineup left incomplete or containing an ineligible player will automatically be populated with the highest-projected eligible player at that position. Exceptions to this rule may be granted solely at the discretion of the Commissioner (e.g., bye weeks with no eligible or droppable replacement players on the roster).
3. **In-Week Substitutions.** A player may be substituted in or out of a starting lineup position at any point up until the scheduled kickoff of that player's game. Once a player's game has begun, that lineup position is locked for the remainder of the week.
4. **One Team Per Manager.** Each league manager may own and control no more than one (1) team. Managing multiple teams under different identities, or on behalf of another person for the purpose of circumventing this rule, is strictly prohibited.
5. **Entry Fees.** All entry fees must be paid in full to the Commissioner prior to the start of the league draft. Managers who have not paid by the stated draft date may be removed from the league at the Commissioner's discretion.
6. **Roster Size & IR.** Each team will carry six (6) bench slots and one (1) injured reserve slot. A player may be placed in the IR slot only while designated as injured, freeing the corresponding bench spot for a free agent signing or trade acquisition to temporarily fill that player's role.
7. **Reporting Violations.** Any suspected violation of league rules must be reported to the Commissioner in a timely manner. Failure to report a known violation may itself be treated as a violation.
8. **Rule Amendments.** These rules may be amended only with a majority vote of league managers, or at the sole discretion of the Commissioner in cases of ambiguity, exploitation, or unforeseen circumstance not addressed herein.

### Article II — Draft Rules

1. **Draft Format.** The draft will be conducted live via Yahoo Fantasy Sports at a time selected by the Commissioner, contingent on the availability of all managers.
2. **Draft Order.** The draft order will be randomly generated once all managers have confirmed their slots and the final list of participating managers has been submitted.
3. **Time Limit Per Pick.** Managers shall have 75 seconds to make each selection. Failure to submit a selection within the allotted time will result in autodraft selecting a player on the manager's behalf.
4. **Draft Attendance.** Managers unable to attend the live draft must designate a proxy or submit a pre-ranked queue in advance. Failure to do so authorizes the league platform's autodraft to select on the manager's behalf.

### Article III — Free Agency & Waiver Rules

1. **Free Agency.** Players not currently on a fantasy roster may be added to a team at any time during the regular season, unless subject to the waiver wire system described below.
2. **Waiver Wire System.** The league operates on a Free Agent Auction Budget (FAAB) system. Rather than claiming players by priority order, each manager bids a portion of their season-long fictional budget on available free agents during the waiver period. The manager who submits the highest bid on a given player is awarded that player, and the winning bid amount is deducted from their remaining FAAB budget for the rest of the season.
3. **Waiver Wire Eligibility**. Players will be added to the waiver wire when they are dropped by a manager in the middle of the week, or once that player's weekly game has begun, except when the player does not have a game that week (e.g., a bye week), in which case they are not added to the waiver wire's weekly pool and remain free agents.
4. **Waiver Processing.** Waiver claims will be processed every Wednesday at approximately 3:00 AM Eastern Time. Claims submitted after processing will be treated as standard free agency additions on a first-come, first-served basis.
5. **FAAB Budget.** Each manager shall be allotted a season-long budget of $1,000 in fictional currency to bid on available free agents. Bids are blind, and the highest bidder is awarded the player; tied bids will be won by the team with the lower current standing in the league.

### Article IV — Trade Rules

1. **Trade Eligibility.** Any manager may propose a trade to any other manager at any time during the designated trading window of the season.
2. **Trade Composition.** Trades must consist exclusively of player(s)-for-player(s) exchanges. No other form of compensation — including but not limited to cash, FAAB dollars, or non-league consideration — will be permitted or recognized.
3. **Trade Deadline.** All trades must be submitted and finalized no later than November 28, 2026. No trades will be processed after this deadline.
4. **Trade Review Period.** All proposed trades are subject to a review period of at least 24 hours following acceptance by both parties, during which any manager may raise a formal dispute.
5. **Expedition of Trades.** Proposed trades may be expedited at the sole discretion of the Commissioner, provided the trade is non-controversial and the requesting manager can demonstrate they would be unduly burdened without expedited processing.
6. **Dispute Process.** Any manager may bring forth a trade dispute by formally alerting the Commissioner in writing. Upon a dispute being raised, all league managers shall be given a reasonable opportunity to provide input or relevant context before a decision is made.
7. **Commissioner Authority.** The Commissioner holds final and binding authority over all disputed trades, including the power to approve, veto, or reverse a trade found to be collusive, lopsided in bad faith, or otherwise contrary to the spirit of fair competition.
8. **Repeat Infractions.** Managers found to have repeatedly engaged in collusive or bad-faith trading may be subject to a season-long suspension of roster and trading privileges, at the Commissioner's discretion.

### Article VI — Standings, Playoffs & Payouts

1. **Regular Season Format.** The regular season shall span 17 weeks, aligning with the NFL regular season schedule.
2. **Playoff Qualification.** Eight (8) teams shall qualify for the playoffs, seeded against the entire league based on final regular-season standing. Ties in the standings shall be broken first by head-to-head regular-season matchup record between the tied teams.
3. **Playoff Format.** The playoffs consist of three (3) rounds with no byes — all eight qualifying teams begin play in the first round. Each round, brackets are reseeded so that the highest remaining seed faces the lowest remaining seed, the second-highest faces the second-lowest, and so on, ensuring the top seeds are rewarded with the most favorable matchups available at each stage.
4. **Toilet Bowl / Consolation Bracket.** A consolation bracket runs alongside the championship bracket for teams that lose in the playoffs. There is currently no "toilet bowl" bracket for the league's lowest-standing teams, though one may be introduced in a future season to keep managers out of playoff contention engaged down the stretch.
5. **Payout Structure.** 1st place: $450. 2nd place: $150. 3rd place: $60. In addition to the cash payout, the league champion is entitled to the annual championship *TGS ShowStopper Ra Ring* and bragging rights for the duration of the yearly cycle.

### Article VII — Conduct & Sportsmanship

1. **Good Faith Participation.** All managers are expected to set competitive lineups and manage their rosters in good faith for the duration of the season, including in games that do not affect their own playoff standing.
2. **Tanking Prohibited.** Intentionally fielding a non-competitive lineup to manipulate standings, opponents' schedules, or draft positioning is prohibited and may be reviewed by the Commissioner.
3. **Respectful Communication.** All league communication shall remain respectful. Harassment, personal attacks, or abusive conduct toward other managers may result in warnings, suspension, or removal from the league at the Commissioner's discretion.
4. **Collusion Prohibited.** Any agreement between managers to manipulate trades, waivers, lineups, or outcomes for mutual benefit outside the bounds of fair competition is strictly prohibited and subject to the penalties described in Article IV.

### Article VIII — Commissioner Authority & League Administration

1. **General Authority.** The Commissioner is responsible for the day-to-day administration of the league, including enforcement of these rules, resolution of disputes, and management of league finances.
2. **Impartiality.** The Commissioner shall act impartially in all rulings, including those involving their own team. Disputes involving the Commissioner's own team may be escalated to a vote of the remaining managers.
3. **Emergency Rulings.** In circumstances not explicitly addressed by this Constitution, the Commissioner may issue a temporary ruling, which shall be subject to review and potential amendment in the following season's constitution.

### Article IX — League Continuity

1. **Returning Manager Priority.** Except in the case of a severe violation, returning managers shall be given priority in the league renewal process.
2. **Replacement Managers.** Once a season has begun, fantasy teams are non-transferable, and managers must remain with their original team for the duration of the season. In the event a manager is no longer able to serve in their role, the Commissioner will take over the manager's team, making only the bare minimum roster moves necessary to preserve fairness in week-to-week competition. Should the absent manager's team go on to win a season prize, that prize will first be offered to the manager's family, and if declined, split among the remaining managers as appropriate.
